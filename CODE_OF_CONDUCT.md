# Code of Conduct

Tenantkit exists to help people in some of the hardest moments of their lives. We want the
project itself to be welcoming, respectful and safe for everyone who works on it, whatever their
background, identity, housing situation, language or experience.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind, patient and respectful, including when people disagree
- Writing for people who are new to the law, to software or to English
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Crediting others' work and sources
- Focusing on what is best for tenants and the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory or demeaning comments of any kind
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention
- Publishing anyone's private information, including details about a tenant's case, without
  explicit permission
- Using the project to advertise legal services or to collect tenants' personal information
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and when
someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through the repository's
**Security → Report a vulnerability** form (choose it even for conduct reports, so the report stays
private), or by contacting a maintainer directly on GitHub. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers will review every report and may respond with a warning, temporary restrictions, or a
permanent ban, depending on what is fair and needed to keep the community safe.
